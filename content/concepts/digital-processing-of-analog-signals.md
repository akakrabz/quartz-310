*Lecture 18 (Mon Oct 12) §3, slides 10–11 · HW7 #4 ([[exams/quiz-2|Quiz 2 page]]) · family: [[problems/digital-filtering-of-analog-signals|Digital filtering of analog signals]]*

> [!abstract] In one breath
> - Chain: $x_a(t)\to$ ideal A/D ($T$) $\to x[n]\to H_d(\omega)\to y[n]\to$ ideal D/A ($T$) $\to y_a(t)$. Three sketches: $X_d$, $Y_d=H_dX_d$, $Y_a$.
> - D/A rule: $Y_a(\Omega)=T\,Y_d(\Omega T)$ on $|\Omega|\lt\frac{\pi}{T}$, zero outside. A digital cutoff $\omega_c$ becomes the analog cutoff $\omega_c/T$ rad/s.
> - The chain is one analog filter $H_a(\Omega)=H_d(\Omega T)$ as long as no foreign copy reaches the passband: $\omega_c\le2\pi-\Omega_{\max}T$, which allows some aliasing.

## 1. The system

$x_a(t)\to$ ideal A/D ($T$) $\to x[n]\to H_d(\omega)\to y[n]\to$ ideal D/A ($T$) $\to y_a(t)$.

The D/A applied to any $y[n]$: $\sum_ny[n]\,g(t-nT)\leftrightarrow Y_d(\Omega T)\,G_a(\Omega)$ ([[concepts/ideal-reconstruction|ideal reconstruction]]), so

> [!key] Ideal D/A in frequency (Lecture 18, slides 10–11)
> $$
> Y_a(\Omega)=\begin{cases}T\,Y_d(\Omega T), & |\Omega|\lt\dfrac{\pi}{T}\\[4pt] 0, & \text{otherwise.}\end{cases}
> $$
> One period of $Y_d$, relabelled $\Omega=\omega/T$, height times $T$.

Three sketches: $X_d$ ([[concepts/sampling|sampling]]), then $Y_d=H_dX_d$ ([[concepts/frequency-response|frequency response]]), then $Y_a$.

> [!key] One analog filter
> If no other spectral copy reaches where $H_d\ne0$ (always when $f_s\gt2B$), the chain is LTI on $x_a$ with
> $$
> H_a(\Omega)=H_d(\Omega T)\quad\text{on }|\Omega|\lt\frac{\pi}{T},\qquad H_d(\omega)=H_a\!\left(\frac{\omega}{T}\right).
> $$
> Digital cutoff $\omega_c$ ↔ analog cutoff $\frac{\omega_c}{T}$ rad/s ($\frac{\omega_c}{2\pi T}$ Hz).

## 2. Example: a semicircle through a low-pass (slides 10–11)

> [!question] Slide 10 — $T=\frac{1}{80}$ s
> $X_a(\Omega)$ is a semicircle of height $1$ out to $\pm60\pi$ rad/s. $H_d(\omega)$ is an ideal low-pass with cutoff $\omega_c=\frac{\pi}{2}$. Sketch (a) $X_d(\omega)$, (b) $Y_d(\omega)$, (c) $Y_a(\Omega)$.

> [!success]- Answer
> **(a)** Edge $60\pi\cdot\frac{1}{80}=\frac{3\pi}{4}$, height $80$. Semicircles at every $2\pi k$; next copy starts at $\frac{5\pi}{4}$: guard band ($80$ Hz above the $60$ Hz Nyquist rate).
>
> **(b)** Keep $|\omega|\le\frac{\pi}{2}$ of every copy: height $80$ at $0$, $\frac{80\sqrt5}{3}\approx59.6$ at the cut.
>
> **(c)** $\Omega=80\,\omega$, height $\times\frac{1}{80}$. Cutoff $\Omega_c=\frac{\pi/2}{1/80}=40\pi$ rad/s:
> $$
> Y_a(\Omega)=\begin{cases}\sqrt{1-(\Omega/60\pi)^2}, & |\Omega|\le40\pi\\ 0, & \text{otherwise,}\end{cases}
> $$
> the original semicircle cut at $\pm40\pi$.

> [!question] Slide 11 — $T=\frac{1}{50}$ s
> Same $X_a$ and $H_d$. Sketch (a) $X_d(\omega)$, (b) $Y_d(\omega)$, (c) $Y_a(\Omega)$.

> [!success]- Answer
> **(a)** Edge $60\pi\cdot\frac{1}{50}=\frac{6\pi}{5}\gt\pi$, height $50$. Copies **overlap on $\frac{4\pi}{5}\le|\omega|\le\frac{6\pi}{5}$** ([[concepts/aliasing|aliasing]]; $50$ Hz below $60$ Hz). The two arcs add: corner at $\frac{4\pi}{5}$ (value $\frac{50\sqrt5}{3}\approx37.3$), maximum $\frac{50\sqrt{11}}{3}\approx55.3$ at $\pm\pi$.
>
> **(b)** $\frac{\pi}{2}\lt\frac{4\pi}{5}$: the overlap is in the stopband. $Y_d$ is the clean top of the central semicircle, height $50$, cut at $\pm\frac{\pi}{2}$ (value $\approx45.5$), repeated every $2\pi$.
>
> **(c)** $\Omega=50\,\omega$, height $\times\frac{1}{50}$: the semicircle cut at $\pm25\pi$ rad/s, height $1$. **Undistorted** despite aliasing.

## 3. Analog cutoff and how much aliasing is allowed

- Same $H_d$, different $T$, different analog cutoff $\omega_c/T$: $40\pi$ vs $25\pi$ rad/s.
- Aliasing is harmless while the foreign copy starts beyond the cutoff: the copy at $2\pi$ begins at $2\pi-\Omega_{\max}T$, so the condition is $\omega_c\le2\pi-\Omega_{\max}T$ (here $\frac{\pi}{2}\le2\pi-60\pi T$, i.e. $T\le\frac{1}{40}$ s).
- The exam recipe (largest $T$ for a wanted low-, high- or band-pass, $f_s\ge f_c+B$, the sketches and the keys) lives on the family page [[problems/digital-filtering-of-analog-signals|Digital filtering of analog signals]]; HW7 #4 (triangle to $50\pi$, $\omega_c=\frac{\pi}{2}$, $T=\frac{1}{50},\frac{1}{40}$ s) is worked on the [[exams/quiz-2|Quiz 2 page]].

> [!trap]
> - The D/A gain $T$ cancels the sampler's $\frac1T$: $Y_a$ has the heights of $X_a$.
> - $Y_a$ is one copy: zero beyond $\pm\frac{\pi}{T}$, no repetition.
> - Analog cutoff is $\omega_c/T$; do not write the digital $\frac{\pi}{2}$ on the $\Omega$ axis.
> - "Below Nyquist, so not LTI" is wrong: compare the passband edge with $2\pi-\Omega_{\max}T$, not $\Omega_{\max}T$ with $\pi$.
> - $Y_d=H_dX_d$ keeps the $\frac1T$ and the $2\pi$-periodicity; signs and zeros of $H_d$ pass through.
