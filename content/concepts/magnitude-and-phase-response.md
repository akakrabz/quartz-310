> [!key] Definition (Lecture 16)
> $$
> \begin{gathered}
> H_d(\omega)=|H_d(\omega)|\,e^{j\angle H_d(\omega)},\\
> |H_d(\omega)|=\Big|\sqrt{H_d(\omega)H_d^*(\omega)}\Big|\ge 0,\qquad -\pi\le\angle H_d(\omega)\le\pi .
> \end{gathered}
> $$
> Magnitude on a linear scale or in dB, $20\log_{10}|H_d(\omega)|$; both plotted on $[-\pi,\pi]$.

> [!recipe] Pull out a linear phase
> $$
> \begin{gathered}
> H_d(\omega)=e^{j(\alpha-M\omega)}A(\omega),\quad A\ \text{real}\\
> \Longrightarrow\qquad |H_d|=|A|,\quad \angle H_d=\alpha-M\omega+\begin{cases}0,&A\gt0\\ \pm\pi,&A\lt0.\end{cases}
> \end{gathered}
> $$

| $H_d(\omega)$ | $\lvert H_d(\omega)\rvert$ | $\angle H_d(\omega)$ |
|---|---|---|
| $2e^{-j\omega}\cos\omega$, $h=\{\underset{\uparrow}{1},0,1\}$ | $2\lvert\cos\omega\rvert$ | $-\omega$, $\pi$ jumps at $\pm\frac{\pi}{2}$ |
| $2je^{-j\omega}\sin\omega$, $h=\{\underset{\uparrow}{1},0,-1\}$ | $2\lvert\sin\omega\rvert$ | $\frac{\pi}{2}-\omega$, plus $-\pi$ for $\omega\lt0$ |
| $3+2e^{-j\omega}+e^{-j2\omega}$ | $\sqrt{14+16\cos\omega+6\cos2\omega}$ | $-\tan^{-1}\frac{2\sin\omega+\sin2\omega}{3+2\cos\omega+\cos2\omega}$ |
| $1+2\cos2\omega$ ([[exams/midterm-2/past-exams/fall-2024\|FA2024 MT2]] #3) | $\lvert1+2\cos2\omega\rvert$ | $0$, or $\pm\pi$ where negative |
| $e^{-j3\omega}(2+2\cos3\omega)$ ([[exams/midterm-2/past-exams/spring-2025\|SP2025 MT2]] #3) | $2+2\cos3\omega$ | $-3\omega$, $2\pi$ wraps only |

- **$2\pi$ wrap:** bookkeeping, the line folded into $[-\pi,\pi]$.
- **$\pm\pi$ jump:** the real amplitude crosses zero, i.e. a zero of $H(z)$ on the unit circle with odd multiplicity. A double zero only touches: $1+2z^{-3}+z^{-6}=(1+z^{-3})^2$ has no jumps.

> [!trap]
> - A magnitude is never negative: $2|\sin\omega|$, not $2\sin\omega$; the sign goes into the phase.
> - A sign change of $\operatorname{Re}\{H_d\}$ is not a $\pi$ jump: $e^{-j2\omega}$ has none.
> - The value is not the delay: $e^{-j2\omega}$ at $\frac{3\pi}{4}$ has principal angle $+\frac{\pi}{2}$; see [[concepts/group-delay|group delay]].
> - Real $h$ ⇒ even magnitude, odd phase; a non-odd phase means complex $h$ ([[exams/midterm-2/past-exams/fall-2023|FA2023 MT2]] #4, [[exams/midterm-2/past-exams/spring-2023|SP2023 MT2]] #6).
