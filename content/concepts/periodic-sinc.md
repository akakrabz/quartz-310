*Lectures 22 (Wed Oct 21) §1.1 and 23 (Fri Oct 23) §1 · family: [[problems/dft-spectral-analysis|DFT spectral analysis]]*

> [!abstract] In one breath
> - A length-$N$ cosine $A\cos(\omega_0n)$ has DTFT $\frac A2C(\omega-\omega_0)+\frac A2C(\omega+\omega_0)$.
> - $C(\omega)=e^{-j\frac{\omega}{2}(N-1)}\frac{\sin(N\omega/2)}{\sin(\omega/2)}$: main lobe of height $N$ and width $\frac{4\pi}{N}$, nulls at $\frac{2\pi k}{N}$, side lobes.
> - Larger $N$: taller, narrower lobe, approaching the impulse pair of an infinite cosine.

## 1. DTFT of a finite-length cosine

$x[n]=A\cos(\omega_0n)$, $0\le n\le N-1$ (notes eq. 1; cosine rather than sine avoids an extra $j$).

> [!derivation]- Eqs. 2–7
> Write $\theta_\mp=\omega\mp\omega_0$. Euler, then the finite geometric sum, then factor the half-angle exponentials:
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> X_d(\omega)&=\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}A\cos(\omega_0n)\,e^{-j\omega n}=\frac A2\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}\left[e^{-j\theta_-n}+e^{-j\theta_+n}\right]\\
> &=\frac A2\left[\frac{1-e^{-j\theta_-N}}{1-e^{-j\theta_-}}+\frac{1-e^{-j\theta_+N}}{1-e^{-j\theta_+}}\right]\\
> &=\frac A2\left[\frac{e^{-j\frac{\theta_-}{2}N}}{e^{-j\frac{\theta_-}{2}}}\cdot\frac{e^{j\frac{\theta_-}{2}N}-e^{-j\frac{\theta_-}{2}N}}{e^{j\frac{\theta_-}{2}}-e^{-j\frac{\theta_-}{2}}}+(\text{same with }\theta_+)\right]\\
> &=\frac A2\left[e^{-j\frac{\theta_-}{2}(N-1)}\frac{\sin\!\left(\frac N2\theta_-\right)}{\sin\!\left(\frac12\theta_-\right)}+e^{-j\frac{\theta_+}{2}(N-1)}\frac{\sin\!\left(\frac N2\theta_+\right)}{\sin\!\left(\frac12\theta_+\right)}\right].
> \end{aligned}
> $$
> Lecture exercise: the same result from the rectangle pair $r[n]\leftrightarrow C(\omega)$ and the modulation property, $\cos(\omega_0n)\,r[n]\leftrightarrow\frac12C(\omega-\omega_0)+\frac12C(\omega+\omega_0)$.

> [!key] Periodic sinc (Lecture 22, eqs. 8–10)
> $$
> C(\omega)=e^{-j\frac{\omega}{2}(N-1)}\,\frac{\sin\!\left(\frac N2\omega\right)}{\sin\!\left(\frac12\omega\right)},\qquad\frac{\sin\!\left(\frac N2\omega\right)}{\sin\!\left(\frac12\omega\right)}=\begin{cases}N, & \omega=0\\[4pt] \dfrac{\sin(N\omega/2)}{\sin(\omega/2)}, & \omega\ne0\end{cases}
> $$
> $$
> X_d(\omega)=\frac A2\,C(\omega-\omega_0)+\frac A2\,C(\omega+\omega_0).
> $$
> - $C(\omega)=0$ at every integer multiple of $\frac{2\pi}{N}$ except $\omega=0$, where it is $N$.
> - $C(\omega)$ is the DTFT of the length-$N$ rectangle $r[n]$ ([[concepts/window-functions|rectangular window]]); its phase is linear, a rectangle centred at $\frac{N-1}{2}$.

## 2. Shape: main lobe and side lobes

- **Main lobe** at $\omega=0$: height $N$, width $\frac{4\pi}{N}$ (nulls at $\pm\frac{2\pi}{N}$), half-width $\frac{2\pi}{N}$.
- **Side lobes** between consecutive nulls $\frac{2\pi k}{N}$: smaller, symmetric; the first is about $-13$ dB ($0.22N$).
- $N\to\infty$: height $\to\infty$, width $\to0$, a Dirac delta, consistent with $\cos(\omega_0n)\leftrightarrow\pi\delta(\omega-\omega_0)+\pi\delta(\omega+\omega_0)$.
- For $A\cos(\omega_0n)$: lobes of height $\frac{AN}{2}$ at $\pm\omega_0$, plus a small cross term from the mirror lobe.

| read off a magnitude plot | gives |
|---|---|
| centre of the main lobe | $\omega_0$ |
| null-to-null width $=\frac{4\pi}{N}$ | $N$ |
| height $=\frac{AN}{2}$ | $A$ |

> [!question] Slide Example 1 — read $\omega_0$, $N$, $A$ off the plot
> $x[n]=A\cos(\omega_0n)$, $0\le n\le N-1$. Part of $\lvert X_d(\omega)\rvert$ is shown: a main lobe of height $64$ centred at $\frac{\pi}{3}$ with nulls at $\frac{5\pi}{24}$ and $\frac{11\pi}{24}$. Determine $\omega_0$, $N$ and $A$.

> [!success]- Answer (inked slide 6)
> - Centre: $\omega_0=\frac{\pi}{3}$.
> - Width $\frac{11\pi}{24}-\frac{5\pi}{24}=\frac{6\pi}{24}=\frac{4\pi}{N}\Rightarrow N=16$.
> - Height $\frac{AN}{2}=\frac{16A}{2}=64\Rightarrow A=8$.
>
> The exact $\lvert X_d(\frac\pi3)\rvert$ is $68$: the mirror term adds $\frac A2\lvert C(\frac{2\pi}{3})\rvert=4$ for $N=16$. Plots and keys read the lobe alone.

> [!trap]
> - $\frac{4\pi}{N}$ is the full null-to-null width; the half-width is $\frac{2\pi}{N}$.
> - A cosine puts $\frac{AN}{2}$ in each lobe; $Ae^{j\omega_0n}$ puts $AN$ in one.
> - The nulls sit every $\frac{2\pi}{N}$, exactly the DFT bin spacing: an on-bin cosine shows no [[concepts/spectral-leakage|leakage]].
> - The course sinc is $\frac{\sin t}{t}$; $C(\omega)$ is a ratio of sines and $2\pi$-periodic.

> [!exam] Midterm 2
> Read $\omega_0$, $N$, $A$ from a lobe; the finite cosine's spectrum is lobes, not impulses.
