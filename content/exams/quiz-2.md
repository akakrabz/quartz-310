> [!key] Quiz 2 formulas
> | | formula |
> |---|---|
> | DTFT and inverse | $X_d(\omega)=\sum_n x[n]\,e^{-j\omega n}$, $\quad x[n]=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}X_d(\omega)\,e^{j\omega n}\,d\omega$; $X_d$ is $2\pi$-periodic |
> | existence | exists if $\sum_n\lvert x[n]\rvert\lt\infty$; equals $X(e^{j\omega})$ only if the ROC contains $\lvert z\rvert=1$; a pole on the circle adds an impulse: $u[n]\leftrightarrow\frac{1}{1-e^{-j\omega}}+\pi\delta(\omega)$ on $[-\pi,\pi]$; $2^nu[n]$ has no DTFT |
> | free values | $X_d(0)=\sum_nx[n]$, $\quad X_d(\pi)=\sum_n(-1)^nx[n]$, $\quad\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}X_d\,d\omega=2\pi\,x[0]$, $\quad\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}\lvert X_d\rvert^2d\omega=2\pi\sum_n\lvert x[n]\rvert^2$ |
> | pairs | $\delta[n-k]\leftrightarrow e^{-j\omega k}$, $\quad a^nu[n]\leftrightarrow\frac{1}{1-ae^{-j\omega}}$ ($\lvert a\rvert\lt1$), $\quad\frac{\sin\omega_cn}{\pi n}\leftrightarrow1$ on $\lvert\omega\rvert\le\omega_c$ (value $\frac{\omega_c}{\pi}$ at $n=0$) |
> | impulses (one period) | $e^{j\omega_0n}\leftrightarrow2\pi\delta(\omega-\omega_0)$, $\quad\cos\omega_0n\leftrightarrow\pi[\delta(\omega-\omega_0)+\delta(\omega+\omega_0)]$, $\quad\sin\omega_0n\leftrightarrow-j\pi[\delta(\omega-\omega_0)-\delta(\omega+\omega_0)]$ |
> | properties | $x[n-k]\leftrightarrow e^{-j\omega k}X_d$, $\;e^{j\omega_0n}x[n]\leftrightarrow X_d(\omega-\omega_0)$, $\;x[-n]\leftrightarrow X_d(-\omega)$, $\;x^*[n]\leftrightarrow X_d^*(-\omega)$, $\;x^*[-n]\leftrightarrow X_d^*(\omega)$, $\;x*h\leftrightarrow X_dH_d$ |
> | real $x$ | $X_d(-\omega)=X_d^*(\omega)$: $\lvert X_d\rvert$ even, $\angle X_d$ odd |
> | CTFT (HW6 #1) | $\delta(t)\leftrightarrow1$, $\;x(at)\leftrightarrow\frac{1}{\lvert a\rvert}X\!\left(\frac{\Omega}{a}\right)$, $\;x(t-t_0)\leftrightarrow e^{-j\Omega t_0}X(\Omega)$, $\;e^{j\Omega_0t}\leftrightarrow2\pi\delta(\Omega-\Omega_0)$, $\;u(t)-u(t-T)\leftrightarrow e^{-j\Omega T/2}\,\frac{2\sin(\Omega T/2)}{\Omega}$ |
> | eigenfunctions | for inputs on all $n$: $e^{j\omega_0n}\mapsto H_d(\omega_0)e^{j\omega_0n}$, $\;c\mapsto H_d(0)\,c$, $\;(-1)^n\mapsto H_d(\pi)(-1)^n$ |
> | real $h$ (check $H_d(-\omega)=H_d^*(\omega)$) | $A\cos(\omega_0n+\theta)\mapsto A\lvert H_d(\omega_0)\rvert\cos\big(\omega_0n+\theta+\angle H_d(\omega_0)\big)$, the same with $\sin$ |
> | complex $h$ | split first: $\cos(\omega_0n+\theta)\mapsto\frac12H_d(\omega_0)e^{j(\omega_0n+\theta)}+\frac12H_d(-\omega_0)e^{-j(\omega_0n+\theta)}$ |
> | magnitude and phase | write $H_d=e^{-jM\omega}R(\omega)$ with $R$ real (antisymmetric taps: $j\,e^{-jM\omega}R$, add $\frac{\pi}{2}$); $\lvert H_d\rvert=\lvert R\rvert$; $\angle H_d=-M\omega$, plus $\pi$ where $R\lt0$, wrapped into $[-\pi,\pi]$ |
> | group delay | $\tau(\omega)=-\frac{d\angle H_d}{d\omega}$ between the jumps; $e^{-jM\omega}R(\omega)$ has $\tau=M$ |
> | ideal A/D | $x[n]=x_a(nT)$, $f_s=\frac1T$; $\omega=\Omega T$; $X_d(\omega)=\frac1T\sum_kX_a\!\left(\frac{\omega-2\pi k}{T}\right)$: copies of height $\frac1T\cdot\max X_a$, edges at $\pm\Omega_{\max}T$, every $2\pi$ |
> | Nyquist | $B=\frac{\Omega_{\max}}{2\pi}$ Hz; criterion $f_s\gt2B$, i.e. $\Omega_{\max}T\lt\pi$; at exactly $f_s=2B$ the copies touch at $\pm\pi$ without overlapping (HW7 #3(c), #4(b)); Nyquist rate $2B$, Nyquist period $\frac{1}{2B}=\frac{\pi}{\Omega_{\max}}$ |
> | ideal D/A | $y_a(t)=\sum_ny[n]\,\mathrm{sinc}\!\left(\frac{\pi(t-nT)}{T}\right)$ with $\mathrm{sinc}(t)=\frac{\sin t}{t}$; in frequency $Y_a(\Omega)=T\,Y_d(\Omega T)$ for $\lvert\Omega\rvert\lt\frac{\pi}{T}$, $0$ outside |
> | A/D → $H_d$ → D/A | $Y_d=H_dX_d$; a digital cutoff $\omega_c$ becomes the analog cutoff $\Omega_c=\frac{\omega_c}{T}$ |
>

> [!question] HW7 #1 — magnitude and phase on $[-\pi,\pi]$
> (a) $y[n]=x[n+2]+x[n-2]$ · (b) $y[n]=x[n]-x[n-4]$ · (c) $y[n]=x[n+2]+2x[n]+x[n-2]$

> [!success]- Solution
> **(a)** $H_d(\omega)=e^{j2\omega}+e^{-j2\omega}=2\cos2\omega$, real. $\lvert H_d\rvert=2\lvert\cos2\omega\rvert$: $2$ at $0,\pm\frac{\pi}{2},\pm\pi$, zeros at $\pm\frac{\pi}{4},\pm\frac{3\pi}{4}$.
> $$
> \angle H_d(\omega)=\begin{cases}0, & \lvert\omega\rvert\lt\frac{\pi}{4}\ \text{or}\ \lvert\omega\rvert\gt\frac{3\pi}{4}\\ \pi, & \frac{\pi}{4}\lt\lvert\omega\rvert\lt\frac{3\pi}{4}\end{cases}
> $$
> **(b)** $H_d(\omega)=1-e^{-j4\omega}=e^{-j2\omega}\left(e^{j2\omega}-e^{-j2\omega}\right)=2\sin(2\omega)\,e^{j(\frac{\pi}{2}-2\omega)}$. $\lvert H_d\rvert=2\lvert\sin2\omega\rvert$: zeros at $0,\pm\frac{\pi}{2},\pm\pi$, peaks of $2$ at $\pm\frac{\pi}{4},\pm\frac{3\pi}{4}$.
> $$
> \angle H_d(\omega)=\begin{cases}-\frac{3\pi}{2}-2\omega, & -\pi\le\omega\lt-\frac{\pi}{2}\\ -\frac{\pi}{2}-2\omega, & -\frac{\pi}{2}\lt\omega\lt0\\ \frac{\pi}{2}-2\omega, & 0\lt\omega\lt\frac{\pi}{2}\\ \frac{3\pi}{2}-2\omega, & \frac{\pi}{2}\lt\omega\le\pi\end{cases}
> $$
> Slope $-2$ (group delay 2), jump of $\pi$ at each zero.
>
> **(c)** $H_d(\omega)=2+2\cos2\omega=4\cos^2\omega\ge0$: $4$ at $0,\pm\pi$, double zero at $\pm\frac{\pi}{2}$. $\angle H_d(\omega)=0$ for all $\omega$, no jump.

> [!question] HW7 #2 — one input–output pair, a new input
> A real-valued LTI system maps $x[n]=2+\cos(\frac{\pi}{3}n-\frac{\pi}{3})+\sin(\frac{3\pi}{4}n)$ to $y[n]=\frac12\cos(\frac{\pi}{3}n)+\cos(\frac{3\pi}{4}n)$. Find the output for $v[n]=1+\sin(-\frac{\pi}{3}n)+\sin(\frac{3\pi}{4}n+\frac{\pi}{4})$.

> [!success]- Solution
> **$H_d$ at the input frequencies.** $2\mapsto0$: $H_d(0)=0$. $\cos(\frac{\pi}{3}n-\frac{\pi}{3})\mapsto\frac12\cos(\frac{\pi}{3}n)$: $H_d(\frac{\pi}{3})=\frac12e^{j\pi/3}$. $\sin(\frac{3\pi}{4}n)\mapsto\cos(\frac{3\pi}{4}n)=\sin(\frac{3\pi}{4}n+\frac{\pi}{2})$: $H_d(\frac{3\pi}{4})=e^{j\pi/2}=j$.
>
> **Apply to $v[n]=1-\sin(\frac{\pi}{3}n)+\sin(\frac{3\pi}{4}n+\frac{\pi}{4})$:** $0$; $-\frac12\sin(\frac{\pi}{3}n+\frac{\pi}{3})$; $\sin(\frac{3\pi}{4}n+\frac{3\pi}{4})=\cos(\frac{3\pi}{4}n+\frac{\pi}{4})$.
> $$
> y_v[n]=-\tfrac12\sin\!\left(\tfrac{\pi}{3}n+\tfrac{\pi}{3}\right)+\cos\!\left(\tfrac{3\pi}{4}n+\tfrac{\pi}{4}\right).
> $$

## Lecture 18 in brief: ideal D/A

- $x_a(t)=\sum_nx[n]\,\mathrm{sinc}\!\left(\frac{\pi(t-nT)}{T}\right)$, course $\mathrm{sinc}(t)=\frac{\sin t}{t}$; passes through every sample.
- In frequency: ideal low-pass of height $T$, cutoff $\frac{\pi}{T}$; exact when $f_s\gt2B$.
- Derivation, the slide 9 sketch and the slide 10–11 system example: [[4-sampling-and-reconstruction/18-ideal-digital-to-analog-conversion|Lecture 18]].

## Practice quiz

> [!question] 1 · DTFT values (L13–14)
> $x[n]=\{3,\ \underset{\uparrow}{-1},\ 2,\ -1\}$ (the arrow marks $n=0$). Without finding $X_d(\omega)$ first, compute (a) $X_d(0)$, (b) $X_d(\pi)$, (c) $\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}X_d(\omega)\,d\omega$, (d) $\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}\lvert X_d(\omega)\rvert^2d\omega$. Then (e) write $X_d(\omega)$.

> [!success]- Solution 1
> (a) $3-1+2-1=3$. (b) $-3-1-2-1=-7$. (c) $2\pi x[0]=-2\pi$. (d) $2\pi(9+1+4+1)=30\pi$. (e) $X_d(\omega)=3e^{j\omega}-1+2e^{-j\omega}-e^{-j2\omega}$.

> [!question] 2 · Inverse DTFTs (L13–14, HW6 #3)
> Find $x[n]$: (a) $X_d(\omega)=3-2e^{-j2\omega}+e^{j3\omega}$; (b) $X_d(\omega)=\sin^2\omega$; (c) $X_d(\omega)=e^{-j2\omega}$ for $\lvert\omega\rvert\le\frac{\pi}{3}$ and $0$ for $\frac{\pi}{3}\lt\lvert\omega\rvert\le\pi$; give $x[2]$ and $x[5]$.

> [!success]- Solution 2
> (a) $x[n]=3\delta[n]-2\delta[n-2]+\delta[n+3]$.
>
> (b) $\sin^2\omega=\frac{1-\cos2\omega}{2}=\frac12-\frac14e^{j2\omega}-\frac14e^{-j2\omega}$, so $x[n]=\frac12\delta[n]-\frac14\delta[n+2]-\frac14\delta[n-2]$.
>
> (c) $x[n]=\frac{\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{3}(n-2)\right)}{\pi(n-2)}$, $x[2]=\frac{\omega_c}{\pi}=\frac13$, $x[5]=\frac{\sin\pi}{3\pi}=0$.

> [!question] 3 · DTFT or z-transform? (L14, HW6 #4–5)
> For each signal, is $X_d(\omega)=X(e^{j\omega})$? Give $X_d(\omega)$ if it exists. (a) $(-\frac12)^nu[n]$ (b) $(-1)^nu[n]$ (c) $(-3)^nu[n]$

> [!success]- Solution 3
> (a) ROC $\lvert z\rvert\gt\frac12$ contains the unit circle: $X_d(\omega)=\frac{1}{1+\frac12e^{-j\omega}}=X(e^{j\omega})$.
>
> (b) ROC $\lvert z\rvert\gt1$ (pole at $z=-1$). $(-1)^nu[n]=e^{j\pi n}u[n]$: $X_d(\omega)=\frac{1}{1+e^{-j\omega}}+\pi\delta(\omega-\pi)$ on $(-\pi,\pi]$, every $2\pi$. Not $X(e^{j\omega})$.
>
> (c) No DTFT; $X(z)=\frac{1}{1+3z^{-1}}$ for $\lvert z\rvert\gt3$.

> [!question] 4 · From one input–output pair (L15, HW7 #2)
> A real-valued LTI system maps $x[n]=3+\cos(\frac{\pi}{2}n)+2\sin(\frac{5\pi}{6}n)$ to $y[n]=6-\sin(\frac{\pi}{2}n)+2\cos(\frac{5\pi}{6}n+\frac{\pi}{4})$. (a) Find the output for $v[n]=-1+4\sin(\frac{\pi}{2}n+\frac{\pi}{3})+\cos(-\frac{5\pi}{6}n+\frac{\pi}{6})$. (b) Can you find the output for $(-1)^n$?

> [!success]- Solution 4
> $H_d(0)=\frac{6}{3}=2$. $\cos(\frac{\pi}{2}n)\mapsto-\sin(\frac{\pi}{2}n)=\cos(\frac{\pi}{2}n+\frac{\pi}{2})$: $H_d(\frac{\pi}{2})=e^{j\pi/2}=j$. $2\sin(\frac{5\pi}{6}n)\mapsto2\cos(\frac{5\pi}{6}n+\frac{\pi}{4})=2\sin(\frac{5\pi}{6}n+\frac{3\pi}{4})$: $H_d(\frac{5\pi}{6})=e^{j3\pi/4}$.
>
> (a) $\cos(-\frac{5\pi}{6}n+\frac{\pi}{6})=\cos(\frac{5\pi}{6}n-\frac{\pi}{6})$:
> $$
> y_v[n]=-2+4\sin\!\left(\tfrac{\pi}{2}n+\tfrac{5\pi}{6}\right)+\cos\!\left(\tfrac{5\pi}{6}n+\tfrac{7\pi}{12}\right).
> $$
> (b) No: $H_d(\pi)$ is unknown.

> [!question] 5 · A complex-valued system (L15, HW6 #7)
> $H_d(\omega)=\omega\,e^{-j\omega}$ for $\lvert\omega\rvert\le\pi$. (a) Is $h[n]$ real? (b) Find the output for $x[n]=3+2\cos(\frac{\pi}{2}n)+2\sin(\frac{\pi}{4}n)$.

> [!success]- Solution 5
> (a) $H_d(-\omega)=-\omega\,e^{j\omega}$, $H_d^*(\omega)=\omega\,e^{j\omega}$: not Hermitian, $h$ not real ($H_d(-\omega)=-H_d^*(\omega)$: purely imaginary).
>
> (b) $H_d(0)=0$ removes the $3$. $2\cos(\frac{\pi}{2}n)=e^{j\pi n/2}+e^{-j\pi n/2}$, $H_d(\frac{\pi}{2})=\frac{\pi}{2}e^{-j\pi/2}=-j\frac{\pi}{2}$, $H_d(-\frac{\pi}{2})=-\frac{\pi}{2}e^{j\pi/2}=-j\frac{\pi}{2}$:
> $$
> -j\tfrac{\pi}{2}\left(e^{j\pi n/2}+e^{-j\pi n/2}\right)=-j\pi\cos\!\left(\tfrac{\pi}{2}n\right).
> $$
> $2\sin(\frac{\pi}{4}n)=-j\,e^{j\pi n/4}+j\,e^{-j\pi n/4}$, $H_d(\pm\frac{\pi}{4})=\pm\frac{\pi}{4}e^{\mp j\pi/4}$:
> $$
> -j\tfrac{\pi}{4}\left(e^{j(\frac{\pi}{4}n-\frac{\pi}{4})}+e^{-j(\frac{\pi}{4}n-\frac{\pi}{4})}\right)=-j\tfrac{\pi}{2}\cos\!\left(\tfrac{\pi}{4}n-\tfrac{\pi}{4}\right).
> $$
> $y[n]=-j\pi\cos(\frac{\pi}{2}n)-j\frac{\pi}{2}\cos(\frac{\pi}{4}n-\frac{\pi}{4})$, purely imaginary.

> [!question] 6 · Magnitude, phase and group delay (L16, HW7 #1)
> Give $\lvert H_d(\omega)\rvert$, a piecewise $\angle H_d(\omega)$ on $[-\pi,\pi]$, and the group delay for (a) $y[n]=x[n]+x[n-1]+x[n-2]$ and (b) $y[n]=x[n]-x[n-6]$.

> [!success]- Solution 6
> (a) $H_d=1+e^{-j\omega}+e^{-j2\omega}=e^{-j\omega}(1+2\cos\omega)$. $\lvert H_d\rvert=\lvert1+2\cos\omega\rvert$: $3$ at $0$, zeros at $\pm\frac{2\pi}{3}$, $1$ at $\pm\pi$.
> $$
> \angle H_d(\omega)=\begin{cases}-\omega-\pi, & -\pi\le\omega\lt-\frac{2\pi}{3}\\ -\omega, & \lvert\omega\rvert\lt\frac{2\pi}{3}\\ -\omega+\pi, & \frac{2\pi}{3}\lt\omega\le\pi\end{cases}

> [!question] 10 · Sinc interpolation (L18; background, not in HW7)
> $y[n]=2\delta[n]-\delta[n-1]$ goes through an ideal D/A with $T=\frac12$ s. Write $y_a(t)$ and find $y_a(0)$, $y_a(\frac12)$, $y_a(1)$ and $y_a(\frac14)$.

> [!success]- Solution 10
> $y_a(t)=2\,\mathrm{sinc}(2\pi t)-\mathrm{sinc}\big(2\pi(t-\frac12)\big)$. $y_a(0)=2$, $y_a(\frac12)=-1$, $y_a(1)=0$, $y_a(\frac14)=2\cdot\frac{\sin(\pi/2)}{\pi/2}-\frac{\sin(-\pi/2)}{-\pi/2}=\frac{4}{\pi}-\frac{2}{\pi}=\frac{2}{\pi}\approx0.64$.

> [!question] True or False (one-line reason)
> 1. If $x[n]$ is real, $\lvert X_d(\omega)\rvert$ is even and $\angle X_d(\omega)$ is odd.
> 2. For every causal LTI system, $H_d(\omega)=H(z)\vert_{z=e^{j\omega}}$.
> 3. If $H_d(\omega)=e^{-j3\omega}$, the system delays every input by 3 samples and its group delay is 3.
> 4. The phase response jumps by $\pi$ at every zero of $\lvert H_d(\omega)\rvert$.
> 5. A signal whose spectrum is nonzero all the way up to 30 Hz (and zero above), sampled at 50 Hz, gives overlapping copies in $X_d(\omega)$.
> 6. In $X_d(\omega)=\frac1T\sum_kX_a\!\left(\frac{\omega-2\pi k}{T}\right)$ the copies are $\frac{2\pi}{T}$ apart.
> 7. In ideal A/D → $H_d$ → ideal D/A, $Y_a(\Omega)=Y_d(\Omega T)$ for $\lvert\Omega\rvert\lt\frac{\pi}{T}$.
> 8. The DTFT of $\cos(\frac{\pi}{4}n)$ on $[-\pi,\pi]$ is $\pi\delta(\omega-\frac{\pi}{4})+\pi\delta(\omega+\frac{\pi}{4})$.

> [!success]- Answers
> T, F, T, F, T, F, F, T.
>
> 1. Hermitian symmetry.
> 2. Only if the ROC contains the unit circle (accumulator, HW6 #5).
> 3. $y[n]=x[n-3]$, $-\frac{d}{d\omega}(-3\omega)=3$.
> 4. Only where the real factor changes sign ($4\cos^2\omega$, HW7 #1(c)).
> 5. Nyquist rate 60 Hz; edge at $2\pi\cdot30\cdot\frac{1}{50}=\frac{6\pi}{5}\gt\pi$.
> 6. $\frac{2\pi}{T}$ on the $\Omega$ axis, $2\pi$ on the $\omega$ axis.
> 7. $Y_a(\Omega)=T\,Y_d(\Omega T)$; otherwise HW7 #4(b) gives $Y_a(0)=50$ instead of $1$.
> 8. Two impulses of area $\pi$, repeating every $2\pi$.
