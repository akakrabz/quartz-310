*Exams · Midterm 1 (L1–11) · Quiz 2 (L13–18) · Midterm 2 (Units 3–5) · fourteen past exams with folded solutions*

> [!recipe] Self-test, then repair
> 1. Finish the unit first: lectures, folded questions, homework.
> 2. Take one recent exam under exam conditions: timer, closed book, handwritten sheet only, no calculator. Skip problems on lectures not yet taught.
> 3. Grade with the folded solutions; tag every lost point with its [[problems/index|problem family]].
> 4. Repair: family recipe and traps, redo the problem cold, one fresh practice problem.
> 5. Use the other exams as a problem bank by family.
> 6. Write your sheet by hand ([[exams/midterm-1/cheat-sheet|example]]).
>
> Key errors are flagged on each exam page and listed in [[0-toolkit/05-errata|errata]].
