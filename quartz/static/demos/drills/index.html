How grading works

Every drill draws fresh random parameters (New variant). Answers are checked tolerantly: fractions such as 9/4 or -5/4, decimals within 10−6, spaces, and optional braces are all accepted; use - or − for minus. Exam-style points count only your first submission of each variant (on the exam you get one shot); resubmitting still gives feedback and, after a wrong attempt, a hint. Opening the worked solution before submitting scores that variant as a blank. True/False uses the exam rule: +2 correct, −1 wrong, 0 skipped. Scores live in memory only and reset when you reload the page.

Unit 3 drills also read π or pi, √3 or sqrt(3), products such as 3√2/2 and 2pi/3, and degrees (60°). Angles are compared modulo 2π, so −π and π are the same answer; decimals are accepted to about three digits.
